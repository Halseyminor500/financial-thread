- Financials: none in a firm email to Halsey beyond the disclosure and exhibits; a production reached him through Provinziano 2/25–2/27/2025 ("now that we have financial records produced"); contents unverified (R-36).
- Equity: the Code names the harm (§ 2120(b)), places the duty on the spouse with access (§ 721(b), § 1100(e), FAM 111), withholds set-aside for inequity alone (§ 2123), and gives the remedies to a "complying party" (§ 2107; Fong). No case found makes a late preliminary declaration, by itself, a ground for sanctions or set-aside.
- "Predicate of service": no; a predicate of judgment and agreement (§§ 2100(c), 2106, 2107(d), 2108, 2110). Since 2012 the clock runs from filing, not service (AB 1406).
