## A. Method

**Documents only.** Every verdict rests on documents: court records, sworn filings, correspondence (by its sender) and
third-party records, each cited by SP-id. AI output is never a reason. That covers Gemini material, AI-drafted packages,
the 81/81B/81C/63K extracts, and census or audit summaries; each points to a document, and only the document counts.
Where Halsey's words state a fact, his words are carried as his account, and the page names the document that would give
that account documented form, and who holds it.

**The §271 finding stands in front of the email-based behaviors.** The court's minute order of 4/21/2026 (EV-003,
SP-0659) found: "The Court finds that Respondent submitted Exhibit F to the Court and analyzed it as if it were an
authentic set of email communications, knowing that it was not." A report that rests on emails "meets that finding in
the Department's hands on day one" (JURISDICTION_SPLIT E.4). So an email-based behavior waits until Exhibit F is answered
at exhibit level by a document (FORENSIC_BRIEF Outcome F; Q-H2).

**What "QUALIFIES — REPORT-READY" would require.** All four of these, together:
1. Every element of one criminal provision in LAW/ shown by such documents. That includes the actor and the act, and
   also the falsity, knowledge, intent and materiality elements, which no document shows for any provision today
   (matrix §1, rule 5).
2. For an email-based behavior, the Exhibit F finding confronted by an exhibit-level document.
3. The one agency with jurisdiction, fixed at source by the place of the act (LAW/jurisdiction_and_intake, final table).
4. The guardrails in §D met.

The institutions have said what they need on top of that. The DA acts after "a proper investigation" by police "and a
criminal finding". The State Bar acts on "specific facts which, if proved, would establish a violation", or on a court's
"written findings".

**No behavior meets that test on 2026-11-01.**
