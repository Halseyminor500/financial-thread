## Surprises and flags (read these)

1. **CCP § 473 has two simultaneously active versions** (both from AB 747, Stats. 2025, ch. 563): Version A (§ 12; operative now; repealed as of 1 Jan 2027) and Version B (§ 13; operative 1 Jan 2027; adds new subd. (e): a motion to vacate a default judgment "that is void for lack of proper service at any time after entry of the judgment"). **Subdivision (d) is identical in both.** The live leginfo page for § 473 shows the same two-entry selection list.
2. **CCP § 1010.6 has no proof-of-service subdivision.** Proof of electronic service is CCP § 1013b (amended by Stats. 2022, ch. 215 (AB 2961), eff. 1 Jan 2023; retrieved as an adjacent section) and rule 2.251(j). Consent by an unrepresented person is § 1010.6(c); mandatory acceptance by a represented person is § 1010.6(b) — the section does not contain a sentence saying service on an unrepresented person without consent is ineffective (rule 2.251(c)(3)(B) says such persons "are to be served by non-electronic methods unless they affirmatively consent").
3. **CCP § 1010.6 is about to change.** AB 540 (Stats. 2026, ch. 263, approved 2026-09-18, no urgency clause) rewrites § 1010.6: it deletes the two-court-day extension for electronic service in (a)(3) and conforms the last sentence of (d); consent subdivisions (b) and (c) are untouched. Expected operative date 1 Jan 2027 (general rule — confirm on the leginfo bill page). The pending text is in the CCP file, labelled NOT CURRENT LAW. The current § 1010.6 is the version amended by Stats. 2024, ch. 151 (AB 2283), eff. 18 Jul 2024.
4. **Rule 2.251:** the Judicial Council page's own history ends at "amended effective January 1, 2022" (edition label "California Rules of Court 2026"); I found no announced amendment for 1 Jan 2027 (search not exhaustive). The rule's last amendment predates the 2024 amendment of § 1010.6 (AB 2283) and the pending 2026 amendment (AB 540).

## Method and limits

- **Verbatim text:** extracted by script from the official XML and written directly into the topic files; every "ELEMENTS" quotation (333 quoted phrases) was machine-checked as a substring of the retrieved text (curly/straight quotes and whitespace normalised) before any file was written.
- **Whitespace/markup:** see "How this file was produced" in each topic file.
- **Elements** are drawn only from the statutory/rule words; no case law, annotations or secondary sources. Where the text is silent on a point that matters (for example whether the procured perjury in PEN 127 must actually be committed, or what "wrongful" means in PEN 518) the file says so rather than filling in.
- **Not retrieved:** sections merely cited by the retrieved text, for example PEN 520, 690.5 and 1170(h); Civil Code 1714.1 and 3294; FAM 2105 and 2110; Gov. Code sections cited in CCP 1010.6; rules 1.6, 2.257 and 3.1300.
- **Local files:** the only files written in the project are the nine files in this folder. Raw downloads and the extraction scripts were staged in the session's temporary scratchpad directory (outside the project), as the harness directs for temporary files.
