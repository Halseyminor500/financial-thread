## 0.4 What the statute's words require for an unrepresented party (verbatim; Part 1)

- **Electronic service needs consent given in a set way.** CCP 1010.6(c)(2): "An unrepresented party may consent to receive electronic service." (c)(3): express consent is given by "(i) Serving a notice on all parties and filing the notice with the court" or "(ii) Manifesting affirmative consent through electronic means with the court or the court's electronic filing service provider, and concurrently providing the party's electronic address with that consent". (c)(3)(ii) adds: "The act of electronic filing shall not be construed as express consent." (c)(4): consent may be withdrawn "by completing and filing with the court the appropriate Judicial Council form."
- **The court rule.** CRC 2.251(c)(3)(B): self-represented parties "are to be served by non-electronic methods unless they affirmatively consent to electronic service."
- **What these words do not say.** CCP 1010.6 does not contain a sentence saying that electronic service on an unrepresented person without consent is ineffective (LAW/INDEX flag 2).
- **Mail.** CCP 1013(a): mail is deposited "addressed to the person on whom it is to be served, at the office address as last given by that person on any document filed in the cause … otherwise at that party's place of residence". "Service is complete at the time of the deposit". Time to respond is extended "20 calendar days if either the place of mailing or the place of address is outside the United States".
- **Motions.** CCP 1005(b): papers "at least 16 court days before the hearing", increased "20 calendar days if either the place of mailing or the place of address is outside the United States".
- **After judgment.** FAM 215(a): no later order "is valid unless any prior notice otherwise required to be given to a party … is served, in the same manner as the notice is otherwise permitted by law to be served, upon the party." 215(b): a motion to modify custody, visitation or child support "may be served … by first-class mail or airmail", and "the proof of service shall include an address verification."
- **DVRO requests.** FAM 243(a): "the respondent shall be personally served". FAM 6340(a)(2)(A): an alternative method only if "after diligent effort, the petitioner has been unable to accomplish personal service, and that there is reason to believe that the restrained party is evading service".
- **Summons outside the United States.** CCP 413.10(c) ends: "These rules are subject to the provisions of the Convention on the "Service Abroad of Judicial and Extrajudicial Documents" in Civil or Commercial Matters (Hague Service Convention)."
- **Not verified at source here:** the Hague Convention's Article 10 and Mexico's declaration under it; the version of CCP 1010.6 in force before 7/18/2024 (Part 1.3).
