## EV-003 — 4/21/2026 — Petitioner's RFO for §271 sanctions filed 12/30/2025 (the Exhibit F finding)
- "The RFO/MTN - Sanctions Attorney Fees filed by Petitioner on December 30, 2025 is denied." Court adopts tentative.
- "Respondent submitted an RFO to the Court on 11/12/25 ... In support of that RFO, Respondent attached a series of purported email communications between him and counsel for Petitioner from 8/10/21 to 7/25/23."
- Quoting the 11/12/25 RFO: "provides a complete and verbatim chronological record of all relevant email communications ... Attached hereto as Exhibit 'F' is the log."
- "In Exhibit F itself, six of the emails are signed "Lance," purportedly written by Petitioner's counsel, Lance Spiegel. At least one email also references Mr. Spiegel's assistant, Janine."
- "According to a sworn declaration from counsel Lance Spiegel, the email correspondence did not in fact occur, and could not have occurred between counsel and Respondent, as they occurred several years before counsel was retained. Moreover, counsel has declared that he never had any assistant named Janine"
- "In response, Respondent does not deny that the email correspondence did not occur." Quoting Respondent: "The purpose of the draft log/summary was to help me organize issues and describe a pattern of repeated requests and nonresponses. I understand now that filing a narrative summary in a document that resembles 'emails' was a mistake because it can be misconstrued." and "I am not claiming that Petitioner's current counsel exchanged the timeline style 'emails' shown in that draft log during 2021–2023." "He is now seeking to withdraw the exhibit."
- FINDINGS: "The Court finds that Respondent submitted Exhibit F to the Court and analyzed it as if it were an authentic set of email communications, knowing that it was not. The Court further finds that Respondent's misrepresentation to the Court was intentional and was designed to mislead the Court into granting his RFO based on fictitious evidence. Because critical evidence in support of the RFO was fabricated, the Court finds that the submission of the RFO was a violation of Family Code Section 271"
- Amount: "the Court does not have sufficient information to rule on the fee portion of this RFO. The issue of 271 sanctions is reserved to the time of trial." "The Court's minute order shall serve as the Order After Hearing."
