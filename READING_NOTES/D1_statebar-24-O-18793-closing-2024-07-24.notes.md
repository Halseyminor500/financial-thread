### (i) What the institution requires / its stated reasons

- "The State Bar’s Office of Chief Trial Counsel has reviewed your complaint against Lance Stephen Spiegel to determine whether there are sufficient grounds to prosecute a possible violation of the State Bar Act and/or Rules of Professional Conduct."
- "Based on our evaluation of the information provided, we are closing your complaint. A lawyer is permitted to pursue the paths in litigation that the lawyer believes are in the best interest of the lawyer’s client, even when those paths conflict with a third party's goals in the litigation. A disagreement between opposing parties as to the merits of a particular legal action, such as what assets belong to each party, does not mean that a lawyer engaged in misconduct. Therefore, we determined that your complaint does not present sufficient facts to support an investigation."
- "Additionally, the issue you raise, such as whether one party is not entitled to assets, is best addressed in the court having jurisdiction over your matter, like a divorce court, where you will have the opportunity to litigate the case before the judge. Additionally, the State Bar cannot make a determination as to the facts and merits of this matter. A civil court having jurisdiction is the appropriate venue to make any legal and factual findings regarding the underlying civil matter, and to issue any remedies."
- "Furthermore, in regard to your allegations that the attorney is committing state and federal crimes by being paid by your wife, whether Lance Stephen Spiegel’s actions constitute criminal conduct is a question the district attorney’s office in the relevant county is most qualified to investigate. Of course, if the attorney is prosecuted and convicted of criminal conduct, OCTC will seek discipline as appropriate."
- "For these reasons, the State Bar is closing this matter."
- "If you have new facts and circumstances that you believe may change our determination to close your complaint, or if you have or receive additional documentation that you wish to provide for our review, you may submit a written statement with the new information to the Intake Unit for review. If you have any questions about this process, you may call Trial Counsel Esther Fallas at 415-538-2037, or send the additional information to esther.fallas@calbar.ca.gov."
- "If you have presented all of the information that you wish to have considered, and you disagree with the decision to close your complaint, you may request that the State Bar’s Complaint Review Unit review your complaint. The Complaint Review Unit will recommend that your complaint be reopened if it determines that further investigation is warranted. To request review by the Complaint Review Unit, you must submit your request in writing, either:"
  - "1) Via email: Within 90 days of the date of this letter, by email to: CRU@calbar.ca.gov; or"
  - "2) Via United States Mail: Post-marked within 90 days of the date of this letter, by United States Mail to:" (The State Bar of California, Complaint Review Unit, Office of General Counsel, 180 Howard Street, San Francisco, CA 94105-1617)
- "If you decide to send new information or documents to this office, the 90-day period will continue to run during the time that this office considers the new material."
- "The State Bar cannot give you legal advice."
