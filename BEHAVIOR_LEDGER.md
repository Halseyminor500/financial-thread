## How to read this ledger

- **Halsey Minor is the victim and the reporting party.** What he reports is carried as **ACCOUNT**, pending the document
  named in each entry. It is never written as a documented fact, and it is never treated as a possible untruth.
- **One entry (`L-nn`) = one dated act.** The act is by Lance Spiegel, his firm or its staff, or it was done to Halsey by
  someone not yet identified and Halsey's account attributes it to Spiegel. Where several papers were served under proofs of
  service with the same date, they form one entry. Acts by other people are listed only as context records (`X-nn`), which
  are not counted.
- **Status.**
  - **DOCUMENTED**: the act appears in the named document. That document's class is one of COURT (court record or filing),
    SWORN (signed under penalty of perjury), CORRESPONDENCE (email or letter, by its sender), or ACCOUNT-record (Halsey's own
    statement, sworn or not).
  - **ACCOUNT**: only Halsey's account shows the act. The entry names the document that would settle it and who holds it.
- **No verdict words.** "Bears on" names element numbers from `LAW/` only, for example "PC 118 el. 3–5". Whether an element
  is met is left to ASSESSMENT_v1.
- **AI material is never evidence.** It appears only as "lead: <file>" pointing to a primary document. This covers:
  - 81/81B/81C/63K, Gemini material, and the "Register of Actions — KEY DATES LOCKED" doc;
  - EV-039/045/056/108 and WP-007;
  - AI-named files, and census or audit extracts.

  - Exhibit F was an AI-generated log that Halsey filed. It originated as drive:1vXbXc01VyXvch2Ae9-JoVsR1LvFkhjQAdh9b6TfCEos,
