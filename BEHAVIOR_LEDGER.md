## How to read this ledger

- **Halsey Minor is the victim and the reporting party.** His account is carried as **ACCOUNT**, pending the document
  named in each entry. It is never written as a documented fact, and it is never treated as a possible untruth.
- **One entry (`L-nn`) = one dated act** by Lance Spiegel, by his firm or its staff, or an act done to Halsey by someone
  not yet identified where Halsey's account attributes it to Spiegel. Acts by other people are listed only as context
  records (`X-nn`). Context records are not counted.
- **Status.** **DOCUMENTED** means the act appears in the document named. Its class is COURT (a court record or filing),
  SWORN (signed under penalty of perjury), CORRESPONDENCE (an email or letter, by its sender), or ACCOUNT-record (Halsey's
  own statement, sworn or not). **ACCOUNT** means Halsey's account only, and the document that would settle it is named
  together with who holds it.
- **No verdict words.** The line "Bears on" names only elements numbered in `LAW/`, for example "PC 132 el. 3, 5". Whether
  an element is met is left to ASSESSMENT_v1.
- **AI material is never evidence.** That covers 81/81B/81C/63K, Gemini material, the "Register of Actions — KEY DATES
  LOCKED" doc, EV-039/045/056/108, WP-007, any AI-named file, census extracts and audit notes that carry extracted text.
  It appears only as "lead: <file>", pointing to a primary document.

  that Halsey filed. Its origin is drive:1vXbXc01VyXvch2Ae9-JoVsR1LvFkhjQAdh9b6TfCEos, created 2025-09-24 06:19:45 UTC
